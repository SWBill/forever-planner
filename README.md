# Forever Legacy Planner

A free planner for your WoW Forever account: characters, professions, race and class spread, and Legacy Points.

**Use it here:** https://swbill.github.io/forever-planner/

- Your plan saves in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Use **Account & data → Export CSV** to keep a backup or move your plan to another browser or PC, and **Import CSV** to load it back.
